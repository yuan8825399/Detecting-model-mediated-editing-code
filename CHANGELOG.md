## 1.0.0

Initial release accompanying the manuscript.

* Corpus: 8,381 records (493 sources × 17), document-level splits, record hashes.
* Detector suite: stylometric, character n-gram, fused, frozen-BERT probe.
* Reliability audits: source isolation, calibration, cross-model/domain/era/intensity
  transfer, dose-response, paired discrimination, false-positive strata.
* Method: PAL-CP (joint provenance lattice + class-conditional conformal sets), with
  coverage verified at five nominal levels and under generator shift.
* 15 tables and 9 figures, each regenerated by a script in `analysis/`.
