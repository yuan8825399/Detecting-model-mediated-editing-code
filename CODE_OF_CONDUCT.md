# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, and do not
publish others' private information. Reports go to the corresponding author; they are
handled confidentially.

Because the corpus consists of published scholarly text, do not use issues or pull requests
to make claims about the integrity of specific authors whose abstracts appear in the data.
